# 05 — Intentions & Agenda for the Business-Strategy Chat

This document states **what the trainer wants to get out of the next chat**, so the strategy assistant knows the mission, the scope, and the constraints. The trainer wrote (paraphrased): *"I'll discuss business strategies I can use **right now** to sell programs and sell clients — either live training from me, or a program they run on their own — plus how/who to approach, pricing, growth, marketing, and social media posts on my personal accounts."*

## Primary objective
Produce **concrete, do-it-now strategies** to start generating clients and revenue from a standing start (one client), using the trainer's existing coaching system.

## Goals for the chat (what success looks like)
1. **Define the offers:** decide what to actually sell — live 1:1, self-run programs, hybrid — and how each is packaged and named.
2. **Pricing:** set real numbers for each offer (and any intro/founding-client pricing to build proof).
3. **Target audience ("who to approach"):** identify the specific group(s) of people to go after first and why, and where to find them.
4. **Acquisition / "how to approach":** concrete outreach and conversion tactics that work at zero/low budget and zero audience (e.g., warm network, local, organic social, referrals).
5. **Marketing & social media:** a practical content plan for the trainer's **personal** accounts (what to post, formats, cadence), including example posts.
6. **Growth:** a sequence — what to do this week, this month, and over the first 90 days — and how to scale from one client toward a sustainable book of business.

## Topics the assistant should be ready to cover
- Offer design and tiering (live vs self-run vs hybrid).
- Pricing models (per-session, packages, monthly programming/coaching retainers, founding-client deals).
- Niche/positioning (who the trainer is best for; specialization vs general).
- Lead generation with no budget and no following (personal network, local outreach, organic content, partnerships/referrals).
- Social media content strategy and **specific example posts/captions** for personal accounts.
- Converting interest into paying clients (consultations, intro offers, scripts).
- Building social proof fast (results, testimonials, before/after using the app's progress data).
- A prioritized action plan (this week / 30 days / 90 days).
- Lightweight ops to enable sales now (how to collect payment, schedule, and onboard given the current toolset).

## Constraints the assistant MUST respect (from the other documents)
- **One client today; no testimonials/results bank yet.** Early plays should generate proof, not assume it.
- **The app is trainer-only.** A "self-run program" is delivered as a **spreadsheet/document**, not a client app. Don't promise a client-facing app experience.
- **No payment/booking system yet** — recommend simple, immediate ways to collect money and schedule.
- **No marketing presence; personal social accounts only** to start.
- **Solo operator** — live training is time-capped; factor scalability into the offer mix.
- Prefer **free/low-cost, high-leverage** tactics suitable for a beginner with limited time and budget.

## Open questions the assistant should ask the trainer early
- Location/market, and in-person radius vs willingness to coach remotely.
- Hours per week available for clients, and target income.
- Any budget for ads/tools, or strictly organic to start.
- Preferred niche or client type (and the trainer's own physique/story as a marketing asset).
- Current personal social following/size and which platforms.
- Comfort level being on camera / posting publicly.

## Desired output style
- **Actionable and specific** (steps, numbers, scripts, example posts) — not generic advice.
- **Sequenced** (what to do first), and **realistic** for a one-client, no-budget, solo starting point.
- Honest about trade-offs (e.g., live = premium but capped; self-run = scalable but currently a document).

## What this chat is NOT for
- It's not for building or changing the web app (that work continues in the original development chat).
- It's not legal/tax/financial advice — flag where a professional is warranted (e.g., waivers, business formation, taxes).
