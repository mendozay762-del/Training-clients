# 04 — App-to-Offerings Bridge: What's Sellable Now

This connects *what the app/trainer can actually do* to *concrete offers that can be sold today*, including the gaps that limit each. It does **not** set prices or strategy — that's the new chat's job — but it gives the strategist an accurate menu of what's deliverable.

> **Read Document 06 first.** Because the trainer has **no certification or insurance** (Texas), the in-person/commercial-gym offers below are **constrained today** — most commercial gyms require cert + insurance to coach paying clients on-site. **Online/remote (Offer B) is the realistic, lowest-risk primary offer right now.**

## Offer A — Live 1:1 personal training (in person)
- **What the client gets:** in-person coaching, every set logged in the app, periodic assessments, and a visible progress trend the trainer shows them.
- **App's role:** full — onboarding, programming, live logging, progress.
- **Strengths:** highest value/touch, easiest to charge a premium for, strongest results and accountability, best for generating testimonials and content.
- **Limits:** consumes the trainer's hours 1:1 → **does not scale**; capped by available time and local geography. **Cert/insurance constraint:** coaching paid clients in a **commercial gym** generally requires certification + insurance (not held) → that venue is effectively blocked today; only the trainer's own space or a client's home is available, and that's **uninsured** (liability risk — see Doc 06).
- **Readiness:** **constrained** — deliverable only outside commercial gyms and while uninsured; not the recommended starting lane.

## Offer B — Remote / self-run program
- **What the client gets:** a structured, individualized program **as a spreadsheet/document**, run on their own, with the trainer reviewing results and updating it each block.
- **App's role:** program design + trainer-side tracking; **the client does not use the app.**
- **Strengths:** **scales** (low time per client), supports recurring revenue (monthly programming), reaches clients beyond the local area, **and avoids the cert/insurance/gym gatekeepers** — the lowest-risk path while uncertified.
- **Limits:** the client-facing artifact is a **sheet, not software**, so it feels less premium than a polished app; accountability depends on the client; results data depends on what the client reports.
- **Readiness:** **deliverable today and the recommended primary offer right now** (caveat: delivery = a document; a true client app/portal is a future build). Still use a waiver + PAR-Q screening (Doc 06).

## Offer C — Hybrid (program + check-ins)
- **What the client gets:** a self-run program plus scheduled check-ins, video form-review, and/or occasional live or virtual sessions.
- **App's role:** programming + tracking; check-ins handled via the trainer's communication log and scheduling.
- **Strengths:** balances scalability and accountability; natural mid-tier between A and B; good for retention.
- **Readiness:** deliverable today.

## Cross-cutting gaps that shape ALL offers
- **No certification or insurance (Texas)** → in-person/commercial-gym coaching is constrained and uninsured (liability risk); online/remote is the safe lane. Getting one accredited cert is the unlock for the in-person/gym path. (Doc 06.)
- **No client login/app** → "self-run/remote" delivery is a document, not an experience. Set client expectations accordingly, or treat a client app as a roadmap item.
- **No payments/booking** → collect money and schedule manually for now (payment link, DM, calendar).
- **Thin social proof** → early offers may need to **build evidence first** (e.g., discounted/founding clients in exchange for testimonials and before/after data).
- **Solo time cap** → the mix of A vs B/C directly determines how much the trainer can earn and how fast.

## Marketing/sales assets the app provides
- **Credible intake + waiver** = looks professional from the first interaction.
- **Progress data** (estimated-1RM trends, PRs, adherence, body-stat changes) = the raw material for **proof-of-results content** and client retention — currently limited to one client, so generating more results is itself an early priority.
- **Systematized programming** = the trainer can take on more clients without disorganization, which de-risks growth.

## How to think about "sell programs vs sell clients" (framing only)
- **"Sell live training"** maps to **Offer A** (and the live half of C): premium, local, time-bound.
- **"Sell a program they run on their own"** maps to **Offer B** (and the program half of C): scalable, location-independent, currently delivered as a document.
- The strategist should help decide the **mix, naming, pricing, and target audience** for these — grounded in the constraints above.
