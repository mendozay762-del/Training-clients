# START HERE — Handoff Pack for the Business-Strategy Chat

## What this pack is
This is a set of **context documents** about (1) a custom web app a solo personal trainer built to run their coaching, and (2) the current state of that trainer's business. It exists so a *separate* assistant — in a new chat focused on **business strategy, sales, pricing, marketing, and social media** — can give grounded, accurate advice without re-explaining everything.

These documents are **context, not strategy.** They describe *what exists and what's true today.* The strategy itself happens in the new chat.

## How to use it
Upload **all six files** to the new chat at the start, and say something like: *"Here's the full context for my personal-training business and the app I built. Use these as the source of truth."* Then begin the strategy discussion.

## TL;DR (read this first)
- A solo personal trainer has built a **private, single-user web app** (used on an iPad) to manage clients, design training programs, log workouts, and track progress.
- **The app is the trainer's tool only — clients cannot log in or see it.** Programs are delivered to clients outside the app (via a spreadsheet/printout).
- The business is **at the very beginning: one (1) real paying-ish client.** (A cousin is not paying, doesn't lift, and isn't in the app — so effectively one client.)
- There is **no pricing set, no payment system, no marketing presence, and no social proof yet.**
- The trainer wants to start **selling**: live 1:1 training and/or self-run programs — and needs help with who to target, pricing, growth, marketing, and social content.

## The documents
1. **00 — START HERE** (this file): how to use the pack + the non-negotiable facts.
2. **01 — Web App Capabilities:** an exhaustive, feature-by-feature reference of what the app does and does not do.
3. **02 — Web App Workflow & Delivery:** how a client moves from onboarding → program → logged sessions → progress, and the exact program-delivery models.
4. **03 — Business Current State:** the honest snapshot — clients, assets, constraints, tools, capacity.
5. **04 — App-to-Offerings Bridge:** how the app maps to specific sellable offers today, and the gaps that limit them.
6. **05 — Next-Chat Intentions & Agenda:** what the trainer wants to achieve in the strategy chat, the topics to cover, and the constraints to respect.
7. **06 — Certification, Licensing, Insurance & Risk:** the Texas / no-certification reality and the legal-insurance-liability constraints the strategy MUST design within. **Read this early — it limits where and how the trainer can sell.**

## Facts the strategy assistant must NOT get wrong
- **Client count = 1.** Do not assume a roster. No testimonials/results bank yet.
- **The app is single-user (trainer only).** Clients do not have accounts, cannot self-log, and cannot view their data in it. "Self-run program" = the client receives a program (spreadsheet/printout) and runs it on their own; they are not using the app.
- **No payments, booking, or billing exist** in the app or the business yet — money would be collected manually (e.g., a payment link) until that's built.
- **No marketing/brand presence is established.** Social media would be on the trainer's *personal* accounts to start.
- **Solo operator.** Live 1:1 is time-bound and doesn't scale; self-run/remote scales better but currently relies on the spreadsheet, not a polished client app.
- Treat any "client-facing app" capability as a **future build**, not something available today.
- **No certification or license; based in Texas; choosing to operate uncertified for now.** Training is unregulated to *operate* in TX, but no cert almost certainly means **no liability insurance**, and **commercial gyms generally require cert + insurance** — so in-person/gym coaching is constrained and **online/remote is the viable lane now.** (Full detail in Document 06.)
- **Side hustle**, aiming to scale modestly.
- **Payments via cash / Zelle** (no card processor planned).
