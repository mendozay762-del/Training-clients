### 1. Acknowledgment of the activity

I, the undersigned (the "Client"), understand that I am voluntarily participating in personal training services provided by [YOUR FULL NAME] (the "Trainer"). These services may include but are not limited to: resistance training, cardiovascular exercise, mobility work, conditioning, nutritional guidance of a general (non-medical) nature, and related fitness instruction.

### 2. Assumption of risk

I understand that physical exercise carries inherent risks, including but not limited to: muscle strain, ligament or tendon injury, joint injury, fracture, cardiovascular events (including heart attack and stroke), heat-related illness, and in rare cases, death. I assume all such risks knowingly and voluntarily.

### 3. Representation of health

I represent that I have either:

- (a) Consulted a physician regarding my participation in this exercise program, **OR**
- (b) Voluntarily chosen not to consult a physician and accept full responsibility for that decision.

I will inform the Trainer immediately of any change in my health status, medications, injuries, or symptoms (including but not limited to chest pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, joint pain, or unusual fatigue) that may arise before, during, or after any session.

### 4. Release of liability

In consideration of being permitted to participate, I release, waive, and discharge the Trainer from any and all liability, claims, demands, actions, and causes of action arising out of any injury, illness, loss, or damage I may suffer in connection with the Trainer's services, **except** where caused by the Trainer's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### 5. Scope of services

I acknowledge that the Trainer is **not** a licensed medical professional, physical therapist, registered dietitian, or psychologist. The Trainer's services do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, prescription, physical therapy, or licensed dietary counseling. For any condition requiring such professional care, I will consult an appropriately licensed provider.

### 6. Photography and records

I understand that the Trainer may keep written records of my training sessions, measurements, goals, and communications for the purpose of providing services. These records will be kept confidential and not shared without my written permission, except as required by law.

[OPTIONAL — delete if not applicable: I [DO / DO NOT] consent to photographs or videos of myself being taken for the purpose of form review and progress tracking. Such media will not be published or shared outside the trainer-client relationship without my separate written consent.]

### 7. Severability

If any provision of this agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect.

### 8. Acknowledgment

I have read this entire document. I understand its terms. I have had the opportunity to ask questions and have any concerns addressed. I sign this voluntarily.

## How to use this template

1. **Get it reviewed.** Send to an attorney in your state. Cost: typically $100–$300 for a quick review of a one-pager.
2. **Customize the bracketed fields** with your full legal name and any state-specific language they advise.
3. **Send to every client before their first session.** Even existing ones — backdate is fine if they sign now.
4. **Keep signed copies.** PDF in a folder on Google Drive labeled `Clients/[Client Name]/Waiver-Signed-[Date].pdf`.
5. **Mirror in the app.** Once the in-app waiver step is built (M2a), the digital acknowledgment supplements but does not replace the signed paper/PDF.

## Why this matters even informally

You don't carry a cert and you're storing health data. Both increase your exposure if something goes wrong. A signed waiver:

- Documents that the client understood the risks.
- Documents that you disclosed your scope of practice (not a medical/PT/RD professional).
- Shifts the standard of liability from ordinary negligence to gross negligence in most states.
- Signals professionalism — clients take training more seriously when they sign one.
